# CIS 106 - Module 3 IPO Charts

All dollar values, exam points, and circle measurements display two decimal places. Discount percent is entered as a decimal (0.15 for 15%) and displayed as a percentage with two decimal places. Numeric inputs use float; names, ticker symbols, makes, and models use strings.

## M3P1 - Stock investment

| Input | Process | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ticker symbol; number of shares; cost per share | amount_invested = number_of_shares * cost_per_share | Ticker symbol; amount invested |

## M3P2 - Weighted exam points

| Input | Process | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Last name; midterm score (0-100); final exam score (0-100) | total_exam_points = midterm_score * 0.40 + final_score * 0.60 | Last name; total exam points |

## M3P3 - Split job payment

| Input | Process | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total amount received | amount_per_person = amount_received / 3 | Amount each of the three people receives |

## M3P4 - Auto discount

| Input | Process | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Make; model; MSRP; discount percent as a decimal | amount_off = msrp * discount_percent; discounted_price = msrp - amount_off | Make; model; MSRP; discount percent; amount off; discounted price |

## M3P5 - Circle area and perimeter

| Input | Process | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circle radius | pi = 3.14; area = pi * radius * radius; perimeter = 2 * pi * radius | Area in square units; perimeter in units |
