M7P5 - Logic

1. Set total discounts to zero. Ask whether to continue.
2. While the answer is Yes (ignoring case and surrounding spaces):
   a. Input quantity and unit price. Extended price = quantity * price.
   b. Use a 25% discount if extended price is greater than 10000; otherwise use 10%.
   c. Discount amount = extended price * discount rate. Total = extended price - discount amount.
   d. Display extended price, discount amount, and total.
   e. Add discount amount to total discounts. Ask whether to enter another order.
3. Display sum of all discounts.
