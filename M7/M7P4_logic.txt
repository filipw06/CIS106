M7P4 - Logic

1. Set total gross pay and employee count to zero. Ask whether to continue.
2. While the answer is Yes (ignoring case and surrounding spaces):
   a. Input last name, hours, and hourly rate.
   b. If hours exceed 40, gross pay = 40 * rate + (hours - 40) * rate * 1.5. Otherwise, gross pay = hours * rate.
   c. Display last name and gross pay.
   d. Add gross pay to total and add one to employee count.
   e. Ask whether to enter another employee.
3. If employee count is positive, average pay = total / count. Otherwise, average pay = zero.
4. Display total gross pay, employee count, and average pay.
