# M11P1: Discount amount and discounted price

## IPO
Input: quantity, unit price, discount rate (percentage).
Process: function computes original total = quantity * price; discount = original total * rate; discounted price = original total - discount.
Output: quantity, unit price, discount amount, discounted price.

## Steps
1. Get the inputs.
2. Call the function.
3. Display the required results.
